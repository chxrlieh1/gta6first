GTA6_First Publisher

GTA6_First Publisher is a private tool used by the GTA 6 First fan page to upload its own edited short videos to its own YouTube channel, @GTA6_First.

It is used only by the channel owner. It does not offer accounts to anyone else, and it does not read, change or delete anything other than the videos it uploads.
