Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

GTA6_First Publisher ("the app") is a private tool that uploads videos to the YouTube channel @gta6_first_1 on behalf of that channel's owner. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, given through Google sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel (youtube.upload), and
  • read basic statistics, such as view counts, for videos the channel has posted (youtube.readonly).

What the app stores

The app stores only the sign-in token Google issues. It is kept in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. The app does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it does not send YouTube data to any third party.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing it makes the app stop working, and the stored token no longer works.
